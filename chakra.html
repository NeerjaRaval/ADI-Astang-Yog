Subtle Energy System

The Seven Chakras

Explore the primary vortexes of energy aligned along your spine from Sahasrara (Crown) down to Muladhara (Root). Harmonizing these energy centers balances your physical body, mind, and cosmic consciousness.

Sahasrara

(Crown Chakra) "Thousand-Folded Lotus" — Portal of cosmic unity
Chakra 1
Location
Crown of Head
Element
Thought / Pure Consciousness
Seed Sound
OM / Silence
Core Essence
Divine Connection, Enlightenment

Sahasrara sits at the crown of the head as the gateway to universal consciousness and divine spirit. It represents our capacity to connect to the transcendent, realize our inner divinity, and experience pure bliss (ananda).

Importance to Balance

Underactive Spiritual cynicism, disconnect from self, rigid skepticism, apathy, depression.
Balanced Spiritual wisdom, feeling connected to the universe, profound peace, liberation.
Overactive Disembodied living, spiritual bypassing, isolation, separation from physical reality.

Balancing Sadhana

Yoga Asanas
Sirsasana (Headstand), Padmasana (Lotus), Savasana (Corpse Pose)
Affirmation
"I am connected to the universal source of light, love, and wisdom."
Crystal & Oil
Clear Quartz & Selenite / Frankincense & Rose
Meditation
Practice silent meditation, focusing on a violet/white lotus above the head.

Ajna

Third Eye Chakra "Command & Perceive" — Seat of intuition and inner sight
Chakra 2
Location
Forehead (between eyebrows)
Element
Light (Jyoti)
Seed Sound
OM
Core Essence
Intuition, Wisdom, Inner Sight

Ajna transcends physical sight, offering inner vision, intuition, wisdom, and cosmic clarity. Operating as the gateway to the subconscious, it helps you see past illusions and connect to the inner truth of all things.

Importance to Balance

Underactive Lack of direction, cynicism, memory issues, narrow-mindedness, feeling lost.
Balanced Intuitive insight, clear vision, mental clarity, creative imagination, wisdom.
Overactive Headaches, nightmares, overthinking, anxiety, hallucinations, living in fantasy.

Balancing Sadhana

Yoga Asanas
Balasana (Child's Pose), Sukhasana (Easy Pose), Adho Mukha Svanasana
Affirmation
"I trust my intuition and see my path with perfect clarity."
Crystal & Oil
Amethyst & Fluorite / Lavender & Frankincense
Meditation
Practice Trataka (candle gazing) or concentrate on an indigo light on your brow.

Vishuddha

Throat Chakra "Especially Pure" — Hub of truth and self-expression
Chakra 3
Location
Base of Throat
Element
Ether / Space (Akasha)
Seed Sound
HAM
Core Essence
Truth, Communication, Expression

Vishuddha oversees your expression and voice. As the center for communication, it enables you to speak your truth honestly and listen to others deeply. Physically, it regulates the thyroid and neck alignment.

Importance to Balance

Underactive Fear of speaking, throat tightness, shyness, hiding your truth, stuttering.
Balanced Clear communication, truthfulness, creative self-expression, empathetic listening.
Overactive Gossiping, talking excessively, interrupting, sharp/aggressive tone, inability to listen.

Balancing Sadhana

Yoga Asanas
Sarvangasana (Shoulderstand), Matsyasana (Fish Pose), Ustrasana
Affirmation
"I express my truth with clarity, love, and courage."
Crystal & Oil
Lapis Lazuli & Sodalite / Eucalyptus & Peppermint
Meditation
Chant HAM while focusing on a spinning sky-blue vortex at your throat.

Anahata

Heart Chakra "Unstruck Sound" — Bridge of compassion and love
Chakra 4
Location
Center of Chest (Heart)
Element
Air (Vayu)
Seed Sound
YAM
Core Essence
Unconditional Love, Empathy, Peace

Anahata resides at the center of the chakra system, acting as the bridge between the lower physical chakras and the higher spiritual ones. It governs love, empathy, compassion, self-acceptance, and deep emotional peace.

Importance to Balance

Underactive Grief, isolation, fear of rejection, inability to forgive, harsh self-judgment.
Balanced Compassionate, forgiving, loving, self-accepting, peaceful connection to all life.
Overactive Co-dependency, boundary-less empathy, possessiveness, people-pleasing.

Balancing Sadhana

Yoga Asanas
Ustrasana (Camel), Bhujangasana (Cobra), Anahatasana
Affirmation
"I give and receive love effortlessly and forgive with ease."
Crystal & Oil
Rose Quartz & Green Jade / Jasmine & Lavender
Meditation
Breathe into your chest, visualizing emerald green light expanding outwards.

Manipura

Solar Plexus Chakra "City of Jewels" — Seat of personal power and will
Chakra 5
Location
Solar Plexus (Stomach area)
Element
Fire (Agni)
Seed Sound
RAM
Core Essence
Willpower, Confidence, Self-Esteem

Manipura is the source of your personal power, fire, willpower, and inner strength. It acts as our internal engine, regulating metabolism and digestion physically, while dictating our ability to make decisions, take action, and hold healthy boundaries mentally.

Importance to Balance

Underactive Low self-esteem, passivity, indecisiveness, victim mentality, sluggish digestion.
Balanced Strong willpower, self-respect, purposeful action, warm personality, healthy boundaries.
Overactive Anger, control issues, workaholism, arrogance, competitive obsession.

Balancing Sadhana

Yoga Asanas
Navasana (Boat), Dhanurasana (Bow), Phalakasana (Plank)
Affirmation
"I stand in my strength and act with courage and conviction."
Crystal & Oil
Tiger's Eye / Lemon, Ginger & Grapefruit
Meditation
Visualize a radiant golden sun at your core, filling you with warm vitality.

Svadhisthana

Sacral Chakra "One's Own Abode" — Center of creativity and flow
Chakra 6
Location
Lower Abdomen (below navel)
Element
Water (Apas)
Seed Sound
VAM
Core Essence
Creativity, Passion, Emotions

Svadhisthana governs our emotions, relationships, sensuality, and creative impulse. Matching the fluidity of its water element, this chakra allows us to flow with life's changes, embrace pleasure responsibly, and connect deeply with others.

Importance to Balance

Underactive Emotional numbness, creative blocks, fear of intimacy, rigidity, low libido.
Balanced Creative flow, healthy relationships, emotional intelligence, vitality, adaptability.
Overactive Emotional instability, codependency, addictive behaviors, drama, overwhelming desire.

Balancing Sadhana

Yoga Asanas
Bhujangasana (Cobra), Baddha Konasana (Bound Angle), Utkata Konasana
Affirmation
"I honor my emotions and allow my creativity to flow freely."
Crystal & Oil
Carnelian / Ylang Ylang, Sandalwood & Orange
Meditation
Visualize an orange lotus blooming below your navel, floating in pristine water.

Muladhara

Root Chakra "Root Support" — The foundation of stability
Chakra 7
Location
Base of Spine (Coccyx)
Element
Earth (Prithvi)
Seed Sound
LAM
Core Essence
Safety, Grounding, Trust

Muladhara anchors us to the physical world, governing our basic survival instincts, financial security, shelter, and feeling of belonging. When balanced, you feel grounded, stable, safe, and confident in your right to exist.

Importance to Balance

Underactive Fear, anxiety, feeling ungrounded, lack of discipline, financial instability.
Balanced Centered, secure, physically strong, stable state of mind, strong survival instinct.
Overactive Materialism, greed, resistance to change, sluggishness, rigid boundaries.

Balancing Sadhana

Yoga Asanas
Tadasana (Mountain), Vrikshasana (Tree), Malasana (Yogi Squat)
Affirmation
"I am safe, grounded, and supported by the Earth."
Crystal & Oil
Red Jasper / Cedarwood & Patchouli
Meditation
Visualize a deep red light at the tailbone connecting down into the earth.
