Yogayurvedic Ecosystem

The Wellness Ecosystem: Practices for Every Body, Age, and Need.

From high-intensity traditional disciplines to clinical therapeutic healing and life-stage support. Find your practice.

Primary Ashtanga Series

Traditional posture sequences paired with Ujjayi breathing and Bandha locks. One-on-one Mysore guidance builds endurance, internal heat, and physical mastery.

Dynamic Vinyasa Flow

Fluid movement synchronized with breath to cultivate cardiovascular stamina and agility. Seamless posture transitions build strength while maintaining a meditative focus.

Power & Core Yoga

High-intensity posture holding focused on building abdominal stability and arm balance strength. Sculpts lean muscle and elevates metabolic energy.

Restorative Yoga

Fully supported floor postures using props to melt deep muscular tension. Calms the sympathetic nervous system and restores deep physical energy.

Yoga Nidra

Guided psychic sleep meditation inducing brainwave states of profound rejuvenation. 30 minutes of practice equals 4 hours of natural restful sleep.

Nada Yoga

Harnessing sacred chants, singing bowls, and sound frequencies to clear mental fog. Dissolves emotional blocks and elevates internal vibration.

Therapeutic Yoga

Target specific ailments, chronic pain, and structural imbalances with customized alignment. Combines gentle movements and breathwork to aid clinical recovery.

Ayurvedic Alignment Yoga

Posture practices customized according to your unique Dosha constitution (Vata, Pitta, Kapha). Harmonizes bodily humors and balances vital energy.

Spine & Joint Recovery

Targeted low-impact postures engineered to decompress spinal vertebrae and lubricate stiff joints. Relieves chronic back pain and restores alignment.

Prenatal & Postnatal Yoga

Safe, supportive practices for expecting and new mothers to strengthen pelvic floor and ease pregnancy. Gently rebuilds core stability and maternal health.

Corporate Desk Relief

Decompress from desk strain and counteract sedentary office posture. Focuses on relieving neck, shoulder, and lower back stiffness while boosting focus.

Senior & Mobility Yoga

Gentle joint-freeing movements and chair-supported variations to preserve balance, agility, and independence. Maintains bone density in a warm setting.
