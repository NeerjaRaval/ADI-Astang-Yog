About ADI Astanga Yoga

Where Ancient Wisdom Meets Purposeful Practice

At ADI Astanga Yoga, we honor the traditional eight-limbed path of Ashtanga yoga as taught by the ancient sages. Our academy is dedicated to guiding every seeker through a progressive physical, mental, and spiritual transformation.

Through rigorous posture practice (Asana), breath regulation (Pranayama), and energy alignment (Chakras), our goal is to cultivate unbreakable inner peace, physical resilience, and self-realization.

1. Foundation

Mastering ethical principles (Yama & Niyama) to build an unwavering foundation.

2. Asana Mastery

Purifying the physical body and nervous system through structured pose sequences.

3. Breath & Prana

Harnessing life-force energy (Pranayama) to steady the mind and dissolve stress.

4. Inner Union

Attaining meditative stillness, expanded awareness, and cosmic harmony.

Meet Our Founders

Guided by lifelong dedication to Ashtanga tradition, Ayurveda, and holistic health.

Dr. Mehul Shah

Yog coach & PhD in Sports

"Yoga is not about touching your toes; it is about what you discover within yourself on the way down."

With a dedicated immersion in traditional Ashtanga Vinyasa, Mehul Shah has guided many practitioners. His teachings focus on precise anatomical alignment, bandha engagement, and preserving the authentic spiritual depth of Ashtanga yoga.
